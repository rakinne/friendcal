## Vision

### 10x Check

The 10x is not more tabs. It is the founding group's first real Friendsgivemas converging in under
two minutes with a stealth hold nobody can detect — the design doc's own success criteria
(`docs/designs/friendcal-hub.md:57,59`). Every candidate was scored against that, not against how
many tabs stop saying "Coming soon".

The reframe that drove this review: **the Apple block is narrower than it looks.**

| Blocked by verification | Not blocked at all |

### Landscape

- **Howbout** already ships shared friend calendars, an instant availability view, plan voting and
  a friends feed, freemium with a Howbout+ tier. A third-party report over ~49 reviews flags an
  aggressive invite-to-unlock barrier as a con. **FriendCal's join-by-link (#5 D3) is the opposite
  posture and is already built** — that is the advantage to press, and it lives entirely in the
  network lane.
- Partiful is text-based invites with date polling.
- A CSCW study of group scheduling tools found early tools failed because they benefited only some
  users, so critical mass was never attained. That argues for the second phone over the fifth tab.
- **Eureka (logged):** conventional product sense files a "Me page" under settings polish, done
  last. Here it inverts — Me is the container for the auth flow's exits (sign out, name when Apple
  withholds it, account deletion, which guideline 5.1.1(v) requires for App Store submissions
  supporting account creation). A Me page is not polish; it is half of shipping Sign in with Apple.
  That coupling is what made Me separable from Groups and Poke, which have no such coupling.
